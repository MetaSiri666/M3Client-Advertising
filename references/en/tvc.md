# TVC Advertising Branch

A TVC must create memory, desire, or brand association within limited time. Focus on how the product causes the story and whether the visual transitions come from the product itself.

## Two branch-specific fixed questions

### How should the product or brand appear?

Offer single-select or mixed options:

1. The product is the hero from the first second and remains visible throughout.
2. Establish the setting and emotion first, then reveal the product in the middle.
3. Integrate the product into human behavior without a hard sell.
4. Tell the brand story through a symbol and reveal the product or logo only at the end.
5. Build the entire visual world from the product's structure, material, or function.
6. Skip — recommend a direction from the product and objective.
7. Other.

### What narrative engine should drive the film?

Rewrite the options for the specific product and include at least four structurally different directions: problem to solution, emotional change in a person, product ritual or sensory experience, transformation between a symbol and a real situation, or one object changing across time or space. Add “Skip — recommend for me” and “Other.” Do not reduce the choices to “warm,” “premium,” or “tech.”

## Visual depth

Select only the questions most useful for this project. Across mixed branches, ask five to seven visual questions in total:

- Does the first frame show an extreme product close-up, a human action, an unexpected event, the complete world, or a brand symbol?
- Which physical quality deserves amplification: shape, material, liquid, structure, sound, sheen, or use action?
- If the concept uses seamless transitions, should shots connect through shape, movement, color, material, or spatial position?
- Do people drive the story, experience the product, provide scale, or remain absent?
- Is the visual world close to daily reality, inside an enlarged product, a symbolic natural world, or a highly graphic stage?
- Should light and color make the product feel fresh, warm, precise, luxurious, energetic, mysterious, or a controlled combination?
- Is the pace one immersive flow, a progressive build, a rapid parallel structure, or slow first and fast later?
- How should product, people, logo, and action information divide attention in the final frame?

## Branch decisions

- When the product has levels, a series, age stages, or a growth pathway, ask whether to show everything or compress it into a few representative stages.
- When the client introduces animals, nature, or cultural imagery, ask for a precise relationship between that image and the product action or audience journey. Do not use it as decoration.
- For food, cosmetics, cars, and durable goods, confirm whether the supplied product material is sufficient for accurate visual reproduction.
- The three concepts must change the creative proposition, such as “the product creates a world,” “a person completes a transformation,” and “one proprietary symbol runs through the film,” rather than merely changing the color grade.
