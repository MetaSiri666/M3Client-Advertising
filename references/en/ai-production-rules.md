# AI Production Boundaries

## All-AI production premise

This is an all-AI advertising workflow. Do not propose arranging new filming of people, locations, or products. The client may freely provide photographs, videos, recordings, product images, interfaces, previous films, and reference images they already possess. These materials may be used for reference, compositing, editing, or consistency control.

When AI cannot produce a shot reliably, prefer shorter shots, simpler actions, a different shot scale, strategic occlusion, compositing with existing materials, post-production text and interface overlays, or a more stylized treatment. Do not default to “reshoot it.”

## Truth and rights

- Replicating a real person, cloning a voice, and using existing footage require the appropriate rights or authorization. Client-supplied material may support concept development, but formal production must record the authorization status.
- Text and images from public websites are research material by default. Do not treat website images as production assets without explicit rights or original files from the client.
- Never invent product functions, performance data, client cases, sales, qualifications, awards, partners, government achievements, scientific conclusions, or testimonials.
- Use client source files and post-production compositing for exact text, numbers, logos, packaging, interfaces, certificates, and official marks. Do not rely on an image model to reproduce them accurately.

## Common production risks

| Risk | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| High identity fidelity across shots from one low-resolution portrait | Produce only a concept-level preview and request several high-resolution angles for formal production |
| Extended lip-sync involving several people | Reduce simultaneous speakers, split into single-speaker sections, or use narration |
| Complex hand actions or precise product interaction | Change the shot scale, divide the action, or composite existing product material |
| Long continuous transformations | Break them into short shots connected by matched composition and post-production transitions |
| Exact text, numbers, or interfaces | Reserve clean areas during generation and add them in post |
| Character or product consistency across shots | Lock references, clothing, viewpoint, lighting, and environment, and allow for selection and retries |
| Medical, financial, educational, or public-policy claims | Use only formal client sources or verified authoritative sources and include them in the review list |

## Internal packaging

When generating Hero Shots, create an internal production pack at the same time. Record shot-level image prompts, video motion directions, negative constraints, reference bindings, character definitions, continuity rules, and compositing tasks. Save it as a separate file and never paste it into the client conversation. Display or export it only when an internal production operator explicitly asks for it.
